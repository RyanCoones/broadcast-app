TOFUGU'S START GUIDE (very important)
https://www.tofugu.com/learn-japanese/
tofugu has a large archive of free resources and tons of articles explaining grammar structures and particles and stuff, its a huge help

ARCHIVE OF RESOURCES
https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1jWPe6FtqnbE4wwk39NxpXuY5kZbwpppb?usp=drive_link
(open on your personal google account) this is insane, its got tons of free textbooks and stuff

KANAPRO
https://kana.pro
really good for memorizing kana, short little quizzes

JLPT SENSEI
https://jlptsensei.com
they have huge lists of vocabulary, grammar, kanji, and practice tests pertaining to each level of japanese proficiency, with tons of example sentences n stuff

JAPANDICT
https://www.japandict.com
this dictionary is pretty good, its got pitch accent which is nice, alot of other dictionaries dont have it

GOO
https://dictionary.goo.ne.jp
when you get more advanced its better to look up definitions and explanations in japanese, youll get a more accurate understanding of the word

RENSHUU
https://www.renshuu.org
theyve got cool practice games and you can learn vocabulary

ANKI
https://apps.ankiweb.net
you can download flashcard decks, uses spaced repetition system, works very well for memorizing words

TADOKU
https://tadoku.org/japanese/book-search/
Free reading practice, very easy (picture books for children are a good place to start)

NHK EASY
https://www3.nhk.or.jp/news/easy/
easy to read news articles
